The path to membership

Masons don’t recruit. You have to ask.

That’s a tradition, not a barrier. Here is what happens after you do.

  1. Reach out

    Send us a note or find us at a public event. Ask whatever you like.

  2. Get to know us

    Join us for dinner or a community event. Membership is a long commitment, so it should be a good fit on both sides.

  3. Petition

    When you’re ready, you complete a petition recommended by members of the lodge.

  4. Visit and ballot

    A few members meet with you, usually at home, to answer questions. The lodge then votes.

  5. The three degrees

    Entered Apprentice, Fellowcraft, Master Mason.

    A mentor walks with you through each one.
