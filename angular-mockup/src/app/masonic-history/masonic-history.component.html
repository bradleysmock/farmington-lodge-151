History of Freemasonry

Origins and Early History

Freemasonry evolved from the guilds of stonemasons who built the castles and cathedrals of the Middle Ages. These skilled craftsmen were "free" to travel between building projects, unlike other craftsmen who were bound to a specific location or lord.

Modern Freemasonry

Modern Freemasonry began with the founding of the first Grand Lodge in London in 1717. From there, it spread throughout Europe and eventually to the Americas, becoming a fraternal organization focused on personal growth, philanthropy, and community service.

Core Values

Brotherly Love

Care and concern for others, tolerance, respect, and understanding

Relief

Charity and community service to mankind

Truth

Honesty, fair dealing, and good moral character

Freemasonry in Michigan

Freemasonry in Michigan dates back to 1764, when a military lodge was established at Detroit. The Grand Lodge of Michigan was first organized in 1826, and reorganized in 1844. Through the years, Michigan Masonry has played a significant role in the state's development.

Notable Michigan Masons have included several governors, senators, and business leaders who helped shape the state's history. Michigan lodges have been particularly active in community service, education, and charitable works throughout the state.
